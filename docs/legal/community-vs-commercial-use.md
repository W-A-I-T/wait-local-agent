# Community and Commercial Use

WAIT Local Agent Community is not restricted by who you are or merely because
your use is commercial. An internal technician working for an employer, an
independent consultant, an MSP or MSSP, and an enterprise may use Community,
provided they follow `AGPL-3.0-only` and the applicable WAIT attribution terms
in [ADDITIONAL_TERMS.md](../../ADDITIONAL_TERMS.md).

## Community obligations

The AGPL includes a network-source obligation. If you modify the program and
let users interact with that modified version over a network, you must offer
those users the corresponding source for the version they are using.

Interactive interfaces containing covered WAIT material must also preserve a
reasonable, visible **Powered by WAIT** notice. The placement may be adapted as
allowed by the additional terms, but the notice must remain visible and must
not misrepresent the origin of the covered material.

The current license applies to the 2.0 development line. Apache License 2.0 is
the historical license of the preserved 1.x line, as described in
[LICENSE_HISTORY.md](../../LICENSE_HISTORY.md).

## What commercial licensing provides

These are commercial rights or services, not gates on who may use Community:

- WAIT MSP Control;
- centralized fleet and multi-client administration;
- proprietary MSP packs;
- consolidated or branded reporting;
- official signed and LTS builds;
- a commercial update channel;
- priority support and SLA commitments;
- NIS2 and European Assurance packs;
- private modifications;
- attribution removal, white-label, or OEM rights; and
- hosted relay or control-plane services.

The specific package depends on an executed agreement. See
[COMMERCIAL_LICENSE.md](../../COMMERCIAL_LICENSE.md) and the [public and
commercial boundary](../concepts/open-core-boundary.md).

## Managed Client

**Managed Client** means one distinct third-party customer organization or
tenant administered through a licensed WAIT commercial pack or WAIT MSP
Control by a service provider.

This definition is a commercial term. It does not restrict Community use or
imply any usage metering in the public runtime.

Under the commercial policy, the licensed MSP Control capability includes four
Managed Clients at no additional charge; the fifth and each subsequent Managed
Client require the applicable paid commercial entitlement. This policy applies
only to the proprietary MSP Control capability and does not gate, meter, count,
degrade, or nag any Community functionality or rights. The precise EULA
language requires review and approval by counsel before publication.

This is product documentation, not legal advice; the executed commercial agreement governs.
