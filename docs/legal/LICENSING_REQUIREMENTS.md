# Licensing requirements for WAIT Local Agent 2.0

> Product requirements, not final legal language. Qualified software counsel must convert these requirements into approved Community and Commercial terms.

## Community route

The Community edition should remain public-source, useful, self-hosted, and local-first. The target model should:

- permit ordinary Community use under the final Community terms;
- preserve required copyright, license, and trademark notices;
- require approved visible attribution such as `Powered by WAIT` where legally appropriate;
- apply the selected source/copyleft obligations to covered modifications and network use;
- define any additional permission for an organization using WAIT for its own internal operations;
- prohibit falsely representing a fork as an official WAIT build; and
- provide no automatic right to proprietary packs, official support, LTS, branding removal, white-labeling, or OEM distribution.

A commercial operator may use the Community route when it fully complies with the final Community license. The product must not claim that every MSP is automatically prohibited from Community if the selected license does not create that restriction.

## Commercial route

A separately executed Commercial license may grant, according to the purchased entitlement:

- private modifications and private plugins;
- an exception from applicable Community source-publication obligations for the licensed deployment;
- operation for third-party managed customers;
- proprietary WAIT packs;
- centralized MSP/fleet functionality;
- official signed and LTS builds;
- premium updates, support, SLA, warranty, or indemnity where contracted;
- advanced Change Governance and assurance capabilities;
- enterprise identity, retention, high-availability, and integration features;
- branding removal or partner/co-branding;
- white-label rights; and
- OEM redistribution rights.

Generic Enterprise purchase must not silently grant complete white-label or OEM rights. Those rights require explicit contract language and entitlements.

## Branding entitlements

The implementation should support explicit states rather than a single ambiguous flag:

```text
wait
co_branded
partner
white_label
```

Community builds retain the approved WAIT attribution. Only an applicable commercial entitlement may remove or replace that attribution.

## Version transition

```text
WAIT Local Agent 1.x
Apache-2.0; previously granted rights remain in force

WAIT Local Agent 2.0+
Planned dual licensing under approved WAIT Community and Commercial terms
```

Do not present the planned 2.0 terms as effective before the approved legal texts, synchronized metadata, and release are complete.
